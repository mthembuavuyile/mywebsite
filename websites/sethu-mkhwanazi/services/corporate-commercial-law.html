Corporate & Commercial Law

Growing businesses need practical legal support that enables rather than hinders progress. We provide business-focused advice on corporate matters, commercial transactions, and regulatory compliance.

Does your business need help with any of these?

Setting up a new company or optimizing business structure
Drafting or reviewing critical commercial contracts
Navigating shareholder disputes or corporate governance issues
Legal due diligence for acquisitions or incoming investments
Ensuring regulatory compliance and commercial risk management
Corporate restructuring, mergers, or winding up business entities

What We Cover

Business-Focused Legal Support

We understand that businesses need legal advice that is practical and commercially aware. We work alongside you to support your business objectives while managing legal risk effectively.

Company Formation

Registration of companies, close corporations, and other business entities with tailored governance structures.

Commercial Contracts

Drafting and review of supply agreements, service contracts, distribution agreements, and key commercial documents.

Shareholder Agreements

Agreements governing relationships between shareholders, including buy-sell provisions, voting rights, and dispute mechanisms.

Due Diligence

Comprehensive legal due diligence for mergers, acquisitions, asset purchases, and investment transactions.

Corporate Governance

Advice on director duties, board procedures, statutory compliance, and alignment with the South African Companies Act.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Strategic legal support for business sales, acquisitions, joint ventures, and corporate restructuring transactions.
