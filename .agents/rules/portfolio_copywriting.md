# AI Agent Instructions: Website & Project Copywriting Rules

## Core Directive
IMPORTANT: Do not use marketing language, agency buzzwords, or exaggerated descriptions.

I am creating a portfolio showing websites I personally built for clients.

Describe each project accurately and plainly. These are **CLIENT WEBSITES**, not necessarily software products, platforms, applications, systems, or digital solutions.

Do NOT upgrade or embellish what the website is.

If I built a normal business website, call it a "business website", "company website", "corporate website", "website", etc. Do not call it a "platform", "solution", "digital ecosystem", "web platform", "technology solution", or anything similar unless it genuinely is one.

Do not invent features, functionality, business processes, results, achievements, community impact, integrations, technologies, or technical complexity that I have not explicitly provided.

Do not infer that something is a platform just because the website contains multiple pages, forms, tables, schedules, or interactive elements.

Do not make ordinary website features sound more sophisticated than they are.

## Required Format & Content
Use plain, factual language that explains:
1. **Who the client is**
2. **What type of website I built**
3. **What the website contains or allows visitors to do**
4. **The actual technologies/features I explicitly provide**

## Forbidden Words & Phrases
Avoid words and phrases such as:
- platform
- ecosystem
- digital solution
- innovative
- cutting-edge
- transformative
- comprehensive
- seamless
- robust
- powerful
- next-generation
- dynamic
- strategic
- end-to-end
- sustainability platform
- community recovery initiatives
- regional hubs
- empowers
- drives impact
- revolutionizes
- leverages
- streamlined

*(unless explicitly provided or factually necessary)*

## Benchmark Example (Amabongo Solutions)

### Real Website Content
The client is Amabongo Glass Recycling in Mkondeni, Pietermaritzburg. They buy unwashed and unsorted scrap glass and bottles for cash, offer collection by truck or bakkie, and have a standard multi-page website with prices (e.g. R0.60/kg for crushed glass), accepted bottle types, a pickup request form, depot location, business hours, and phone/WhatsApp links.

### ❌ WRONG (Embellished / What NOT to do):
> *"Environmental sustainability and glass waste recovery platform. Details community recovery initiatives, recycling depot locations, container distribution, and collection schedules across regional hubs."*

### ✅ CORRECT (Plain, Factual Developer Portfolio):
> *"Company website for a commercial glass recycling depot in Pietermaritzburg. Displays glass purchase prices, accepted bottle categories, pickup request forms, and depot contact details."*

## Tone
- Do not write like a marketing agency, LinkedIn post, case study, press release, or SaaS landing page.
- Write like a developer's portfolio: factual, concise, straightforward, and honest.
- Most importantly: **NEVER invent or embellish information to make the project sound more impressive.**
